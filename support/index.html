Help with the Acade.me app

Need help? Email petro.benjamin@yahoo.com with your device, app version (Settings), and what happened. Screenshots help.

Most answers are also inside the app: Settings → Help → How do I…? is a searchable guide to every feature, with a Show me button that takes you there.

Getting papers in

  • From Files: tap Import Papers and pick one or many PDFs.
  • From Safari, Mail, or any app: open the PDF, tap Share, and choose Acade.me. If Acade.me isn't in the row of apps, tap More and turn it on. With more than one library, the app asks which library each paper belongs to.
  • A link that opens a web page (a journal's article page) can't be imported — open the PDF itself, then share that.

A paper shows "No match found"

Some PDFs carry no readable DOI or title — scans and first-page previews especially. Press and hold the paper and choose Enter DOI… or Paste citation…; it is identified from that. A wrong match can be fixed the same way with Correct identity….

Citations

Press and hold a paper for Copy citation (ACS, APA, Vancouver, or IEEE) or Export (BibTeX or RIS, for Zotero, EndNote, Overleaf, and Word). Collections and whole libraries export from their share menus. Citation files from EndNote or Zotero can be imported from the library menu to identify the PDFs you already have.

Your data

Everything — your PDFs, notes, highlights, and collections — is stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your library; export your citations (with PDFs) first if you want to keep them. See the privacy policy for exactly what the app sends over the network and why.
