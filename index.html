Acade.me Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

The short version: Acade.me collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours. Your papers, your notes, and your reading history live only on your device — and, if you turn on sync, in a cloud folder you choose.

What Acade.me stores, and where

Everything the app manages — your imported PDF files, the library database, collections, read states, and settings — is stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage. We operate no backend: nothing you do in Acade.me is transmitted to us, because there is no "us" to transmit to. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device, and why

Acade.me uses the network only for these purposes:

  • Identifying papers. To find out what a PDF is, the app queries public bibliographic registries — Crossref, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, and (for PubMed content) NCBI. The only information sent is a public identifier found inside the document or its metadata (such as a DOI or arXiv ID) or a title string. These requests contain nothing about you.
  • Related papers you ask for. When you tap Find related papers, the app sends the public identifiers of papers in your library (their DOI or registry ID) to Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex to look up works connected to them — references, related works, and citing papers. Nothing else accompanies them.
  • Searches you run. When you use Find New to search for papers, the search text and the fields you select are sent to the registry being searched — Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex, whichever suits the search or is available — exactly as with any search engine. Nothing else accompanies them.
  • Downloads you request. If you have entered an Elsevier or NCBI key in Settings, you can tap Download PDF on a suggested paper or search result. The app then contacts legal open-access sources (such as arXiv, OpenAlex, PubMed Central, Unpaywall, or a publisher's official API) to fetch that specific document.
  • Links you share to Acade.me. When you share a web page's link to Acade.me (for example from Safari), the app downloads the PDF from that address so it can import it. Nothing else accompanies the request. A shared PDF file is imported directly and never leaves your device.
  • Abstracts of papers you preview. When you open a suggested paper or a search result to read its abstract, the app sends that paper's public identifier (its DOI or registry ID) to OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar to fetch the abstract. Nothing else accompanies it.

Responses are cached on your device so repeat lookups don't leave it at all.

Translation

When you translate an abstract or a page, the text is translated on your device by the operating system's own translator — Apple Translate on iPhone and iPad, Google ML Kit on Android. The text is never sent anywhere. The first time you use a language, your device downloads that language from Apple or Google, as it would for any app; that download contains nothing of yours. Translations are kept on your device and always labelled as machine translations.

Sync across your devices (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on. When you choose a sync folder in Settings, Acade.me copies your library — PDFs, paper details, highlights and notes, reading states, collections, and writing projects — into that folder, in the cloud storage you picked (for example iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or Google Drive), and reads your other devices' copies from it. The folder is yours: its contents are handled by your cloud provider under its own privacy policy and your account with it. Acade.me runs no server and never receives this data. Stopping sync deletes nothing; to remove the synced copy, delete the folder in your cloud storage.

Optional API keys you provide

The Settings screen lets you enter your own API credentials (for example a Semantic Scholar key, an Elsevier institutional key, or a contact email used by services like Unpaywall and Crossref's polite pool). Every one of these is optional — the app is fully functional with none of them. Each credential is stored only on your device and is sent only to its own service, with each field disclosing exactly where it goes. They are never sent to us or to any other party.

Acade.me for Word

The optional add-in for Microsoft Word runs inside Word on your computer. The library file you open in it (exported from the app) is kept in Word's add-in storage on that computer, and the works you cite are saved inside your document so its citations can be refreshed anywhere. The add-in has no server and sends nothing to us or anyone else; Word loads its program files from this website and Microsoft's Office.js library from Microsoft, as every Word add-in does.

What Acade.me does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting by us.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, sharing, or transfer of data — we never possess any.
  • No AI processing of your library; the app's organization is deterministic, on-device logic.

Children

Acade.me is a research tool intended for general audiences. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what leaves your device, this policy will be updated first and the change will be disclosed in the app before it takes effect.
